A toy model of Picnic's incentive loop

picnic-incentive

Eight settlers have six ACTs to mine enough lithium to charge cryopods before the solar storm hits. Anyone who doesn't eat dies, and if there are more settlers than charged pods, a lottery decides who gets a pod. Stickers are the reward. The only thing that changes between versions is how Stickers are paid out.

Version v1 pays a Sticker for every ore delivered. RECYCLE takes ore out of the stockpile and hands it straight back in: it counts as a delivery but adds no lithium. Use the replay to see which settlers find that trick, and how many lives it costs.
